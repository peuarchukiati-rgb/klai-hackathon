10
Post-AGI Era · Why Now

AGI doesn't replace the worker.
It replaces the interface.

Markdown is the lingua franca

ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, MCP, every IDE, every note app — all read it. Markdown is the only format that survives the next 10 years of AI churn.

The bottleneck moved

Pre-AGI: AI was the bottleneck.
Post-AGI: how you ask is the bottleneck. klai standardizes the asking.

Knowledge workers ≠ typers

The job becomes orchestration. The unit of work becomes a .md you hand off — to AI or to humans.

Wellness becomes the moat

When AI does everyone's work, who shows up rested wins. klai is the practice that makes rest possible.
