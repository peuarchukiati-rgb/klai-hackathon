## Production notes (for Peak + Dodo)

- **Total runtime target:** 4:50 (give 10s safety buffer under 5:00 cap)

# === DELIVERY NOTES ===

## Where to slow down

- **0:08–0:32** Doctor A intro — slow, almost whispered. This is the heart.
- **1:15–1:35** Reframe — almost like reading a poem. Land each line.
- **4:25–4:50** Close — same pace as opening. Bookend.

## Where to speed up

- **0:32–0:50** Paradox stats — staccato. Numbers hit harder fast.
- **0:50–1:15** Hidden cost — same. Build pressure.
- **2:15–3:30** Demo — let visuals carry, voice is supporting.

## Pause discipline (where to leave silence)

- After "She forgot to breathe." (slide 2)
- After "The time never came back." (slide 3)
- After "...the time to take care of yourself." (slide 5)
- After "Six hours come back. Every. Single. Day." (slide 7)
- After "but she'll show up rested." (slide 12)

## DO NOT

- Don't say "revolutionary," "game-changer," "synergy," "AI-powered."
- Don't apologize for being early-stage.
- Don't oversell — the human moments do the work.
- Don't read stats fast — let numbers land.

# === BACKUP / Q&A ANSWERS ===

For live pitch round (top 5). Have these ready:

### "Is data real or mocked?"
> "Mocked for the demo. The UX, the markdown logic, the GPT-builder flow — all working. Real device APIs are next phase: Apple Health, Oura, Garmin all have public OAuth."

### "Why Markdown and not JSON or structured DB?"
> "Because the endpoint is a human. Markdown pastes into Notion, Slack, Obsidian. Every AI agent reads it. If klai disappears tomorrow, your dot-md files still work."

### "How is this different from Notion or Obsidian?"
> "Notion stores. Obsidian links. klai *moves work between people and AI*. It's not a knowledge base. It's a communication standard."

### "Privacy?"
> "Data lives in the user's account. Scopes are explicit. Revocable any time. We don't sell. HIPAA-aligned roadmap."

### "Business model?"
> "Free tier for personal: ten dot-md generations per day. Pro at one-ninety-nine baht per month. Team tier custom. The practice (open-source minion library) stays free forever."

### "Why Thailand?"
> "Thai work culture has *kreng-jai* — the cost of asking. Markdown removes that cost. The protocol is universal but the empathy is local."

### "How do you grow?"
> "Loop-by-loop. One HR onboarding loop saves a company six hours per hire. They tell their network. Markdown is viral by design — it's just text."
